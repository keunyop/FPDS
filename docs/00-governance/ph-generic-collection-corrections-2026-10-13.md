First losses and shared changes:

- PH country activation/catalog existed, but executable country/type profiles did
  not. Market v8 defines explicit PH contracts and preserves conditional checking
  transactions, deposit early access/consequences and credit-line security.
- Shared currency patterns lacked PHP/Php and the printed peso sign; HTML leaf
  parsing also dropped a superscript currency before an amount. Explicit owned
  currency records now survive. Bare P/pesos and missing/conflicting currencies
  remain unproven; there is no new country default. PHP file extensions and URL
  query/path tokens cannot establish denomination.
- Identity/currency failures were absent from acquisition missing fields, and an
  undefined contract looked like no missing financial fact. Both now have explicit
  diagnostics; undefined contracts stop without spending on futile extra research.
- Named card pricing lost primary/secondary fee ownership, explicit monthly and
  annual units and actual purchase/payment conditions. Complete named tables now
  require native name/H1/title/route/issuer proof and remain atomic through the real
  chunk builder, bounded grounding and all final origin/financial gates.
- CAD/USD annual-fee bands rejected verified PHP 2,500/2,800/4,000 as implausible.
  Native money bounds now belong to their currency; no FX rate or foreign-currency
  threshold is invented. Exact source proof and finite/nonnegative types still apply.
  Checking Public fee alignment also uses the shared currency bands/prefixes and
  preserves integer trailing zeros (PHP1000 cannot become PHP1).
- Complete loan cost disclosures were split; uniquely defined **/*** note markers
  were mistaken for masked prices. Full APR bounds, fees, example assumptions and
  literal repayment bounds now survive, with independent body-name/SEO/leaf-route
  proof for a marketing H1. Actual masks and incomplete/conflicting notes exclude.
- Estimated amortization/repayment tables and average Annual Contractual Rates
  cannot become unconditional lending-rate scalars, including excerpts ending
  before the explanatory footnote. A current UnionBank regression reproduces the
  former false acceptance and now excludes it.

All fixes are shared paths, with current official-byte fixtures and other issuer,
currency, product-type, ownership, missing-condition, mask and failure regressions.
No bank-specific approval switch, weaker essential, manual review or permanent
recovery menu/scheduler is added. Prompts, parser v29/process v20, market v8 and
