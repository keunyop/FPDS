## Same-evidence optional facts and unknowns

- All five CAD plans include Interac e-Transfer sending, as stated by their own feature cells or the named fee schedule. This does not imply unlimited sending amounts or free unrelated transfers. Structural local replay preserves explicit inclusion for Practical, Plus, Performance and Premium; Blue's old multi-product excerpt remains omitted when it cannot meet the full-context gate.
- Plus, Performance and Premium have the qualified monthly-fee waiver described above. Store exact source prose under the existing waiver field; neither the waiver threshold nor the alternative waived fee replaces the base monthly fee.
- Performance includes one non-BMO ATM withdrawal in Canada per month; subsequent Canadian withdrawals cost $2 and withdrawals outside Canada cost $5. Premium has unlimited included withdrawals in those fee-schedule rows. Operator surcharges remain possible. These are channel-specific facts, not ordinary `transaction_fee` or excess-cost substitutes.
- The current PDF lists standard overdraft protection at $5/month for non-Premium plans and includes it for Premium, subject to approval, applicable regional rules and separate overdraft interest. Preserve conditions; an overdraft-service fee is not a deposit interest rate or unconditional free borrowing.
- Premium includes one order of 25 personalized cheques per calendar year with limited styles. Its eligible credit-card rebate is up to $150/year; Performance's is up to $40. A rebate is not the Chequing product's annual fee. These are qualified descriptions, not newly added scalar fields.
- Blue Rewards' 4,000 balance benefit earns points. Do not reinterpret points as a percentage rate or a waived monthly fee. Welcome offers have separate conditions and are not required for comparison readiness.
- Unproved opening deposit, account deposit-rate basis, interest calculation/payment frequency and other optional details remain unknown/omitted. No extra retries, searches or review were added solely to complete optional fields. No UI or canonical schema extension was introduced.

1. **Essential disclosure link was displaced by navigation.** The old link extractor's 256-link ceiling was exhausted before the directly linked fee PDF. On the actual saved USD HTML, the old companion plan selected only the banking-agreements HTML despite the essential PDF anchor. The new extractor uses main-content disclosure links and the anchor's accessible label to displace ordinary navigation inside the existing 256 total / 64 priority ceilings. The existing per-detail two-companion and scope-wide 48-source limits, host allowlist, country checks and safe fetch remain unchanged. No new search/retry budget or bank exception is added.
2. **Meaning validation missed real monthly allowance rows.** Captured parser-v6 cells have an explicit own column and `Transactions per month` followed by 12/25/Unlimited. The shared gate now accepts only a single exact row/value, preserving the named header and full conditions. Duplicate, conditional, negated, channel-only and conflicting rows fail. Fee-waiver header amounts do not become transaction counts.
3. **The model was asked to rediscover already explicit table facts.** Some model notes identify the correct fee/allowance but the receipts reject synthesized quotations or unproved field meaning. A model summary is not proof. The worker now proves exact owned named-column fields before its existing single model pass, with document/snapshot/parse/bank/country/language identity and the same final financial contract. Proven fields and whole source quotes enter `verified_captured_fields`; reconstructed headers/values are forbidden. The unchanged model pass can focus on unresolved essentials and same-evidence optional facts. Contradictory cells yield no deterministic allowance.
4. **Ancillary CAD/USD benefits contaminated main-account denomination.** The currency classifier now distinguishes complete, narrowly recognized additional/savings-account and preferred FX benefit clauses from main-account denomination. Original evidence remains intact. Actual USD account identity, USD fee, lead-account conditions, unknown markets and unresolved fragments still block the approved country default. The Performance exclusion demonstrates that the rule was not broadened to erase an uncertain truncated foreign cue.
5. **Complete fee/transaction prose was misclassified.** A complete amount-first monthly fee card with a separately labelled rewards bundle can prove its own positive fee. Attached fee conditions, duplicate fee cards and zero guesses fail. An explicit account-wide unlimited declaration is grounded only for the exact owned product; a complete sentence defining when money-out movements count as a transaction is distinguished from actual eligibility/waiver conditions. Channel-only, negated and finite-count conflicts still fail. Shared extraction and comparison/normalization instructions match these rules.
6. **Companion slots could prioritize boilerplate over costs.** The existing eight companion slots now prioritize supplied named-product pricing/excess-cost passages, keeping the 24-chunk / 43,200-character payload ceilings. Selection is not acceptance; all applicability/origin gates remain mandatory. No additional source/model calls are introduced for optional coverage.
