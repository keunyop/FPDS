## Why the ordinary collection missed existing proof

1. **DOM ownership:** the CMS wraps a table in a section without an H heading.
   Its real group name and complete terms are div/paragraph siblings. The previous
   native section reader skipped these records. Savings' numeric rate cell also
   relied on the percent unit in its header, which normalization did not retain.
2. **Distinct financial meanings:** one GIC family includes long/short terms,
   multiple interest-payment columns, cashable products and registered variants.
   The previous family heuristic mixed term groups and generated day counts for
   month/year labels. The strict financial gate correctly discarded unproven
   rates/access facts; weakening that gate would publish inaccurate comparisons.
3. **Actual identity/artifact shape:** the real GIC SEO title is “Products”, its
   H1 includes an explanatory acronym and line breaks, and a separate price
   widget is also H1. Existing tests supplied a cleaner identity. The original
   metadata reproduced the failed split and now has its own regression. New
   product-name creation also previously lost the expansion metadata.
4. **Acquisition diagnosis:** essential research assessed the unresolved family
   even when complete named variants existed. Savings spent both research waves
   on legal/digital-access material, while GIC found no useful later lead. Those
   calls could not repair a parser/normalization loss in already captured pricing.
5. **Verified optional facts:** mapping the same full sentence as two field values
   triggered duplicate-page-copy suppression. Daily calculation and monthly
   payment now retain their separate literal phrases and the full proof.

These are demonstrated representation/binding problems. They do not establish
that any particular model response would reliably resolve other unknown products.

## Shared correction and boundaries

Parser v14 emits bounded atomic named deposit rows, owned term tables, complete
local notes and literal observed product links. The ordinary 900-character chunk
setting preserves these financial records atomically up to the existing 6,400
limit. Oversized tails, unresolved notes and partial grids fail closed.

The shared reader requires the exact percent header and annual basis. Explicit
Annual/Semi Annual/Monthly columns stay together; the Annual column supplies an
annual schedule only when the same owned record proves per-annum calculation.
Day ranges and month/year labels stay literal. No equivalent day count or missing
rate is generated. Distinct named GIC groups enter the existing sibling expansion
and retain the companion's actual document/snapshot/chunk, including stored-origin
lookup. No neighbouring family optional fact is donated to a sibling.

Native identity compares the complete captured name/main section with its route,
including ordinary singular/plural words and a parenthetical acronym only when
it matches the full name's initials. Numeric price headings are not product
identities. Two named headings, unmatched acronyms and registered/cashable
qualifiers remain distinct. This matching never changes the stored name or quote.

A complete unconditional account-wide “no fees” declaration may prove zero
monthly fee. Opening/transfer fees, balance conditions, introductory periods,
later same-product qualifications and linked notes cannot. A separately named
registered-account sentence does not qualify the explicitly non-registered
account, while later global charges still block. Sentence-ending periods after
money are punctuation; decimal fragments and different amounts are rejected.

Acquisition diagnostics use the same shared sibling expansion and financial
sanitizer. Incomplete/invalid raw variants cannot disappear to stop research.
The final normalization, current stored origins, taxonomy, routing and promotion
checks remain independent requirements. Shared model instructions/cache/process
version were updated together. Process is `2026-10-07-owned-deposit-table-v5`;
accuracy/market-profile requirements, capture/planner budgets, security approvals,
currency defaults and automatic exclusion are unchanged. No recovery feature,
menu, scheduler or manual product review was introduced.
